# 🚦 TwinFlow AI  
### 🧠 Autonomous Urban Traffic Intelligence System  
### 🔥 Decode SIH 2026 – AI for Smart Cities, Mobility & Infrastructure  

---

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Urban traffic systems today are:

- ❌ Static (fixed signal timings)  
- ❌ Reactive (respond after congestion happens)  
- ❌ Isolated (junctions don’t coordinate)  
- ❌ Inefficient (wasted fuel, time, emissions)  

### Result:
- 🚗 Traffic jams  
- ⛽ Fuel wastage  
- 🌫️ Increased pollution  
- 🚑 Delayed emergency response  

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**TwinFlow AI** is an **AI-powered Urban Digital Twin platform** that:

- Predicts traffic before it happens  
- Simulates future city scenarios  
- Enables **AI-to-AI negotiation between traffic signals**  
- Recommends optimal speed, routes, and signal strategies  
- Optimizes traffic flow in real-time  

---

# 🚀 Core Innovations

## 🧠 1. Autonomous Junction Negotiation Engine (AJNE)

> Every traffic signal is an **AI Agent**

- Junctions communicate with each other  
- Share:
  - Vehicle count  
  - Queue length  
  - Emergency status  
  - Predicted traffic  
- A **Master AI** decides optimal signal timings  

👉 From isolated signals → **Collaborative Intelligence**

---

## 🌊 2. Ripple Intelligence Engine

> One signal change affects the entire city  

- Simulates traffic impact for:
  - 5 minutes  
  - 10 minutes  
  - 20 minutes  
  - 30 minutes  

👉 Like **weather forecasting for traffic**

---

## 🚗 3. Adaptive Velocity Engine

- Suggests:
  - Optimal speed  
  - Best lane  
  - Departure timing  

- Reduces:
  - Waiting time  
  - Fuel usage  
  - CO₂ emissions  

---

## 🏙️ 4. Real-Time Digital Twin

- Live visualization of:
  - Roads  
  - Signals  
  - Vehicles  
  - Emergency routes  

- Supports:
  - Simulation  
  - Replay  
  - What-if analysis
